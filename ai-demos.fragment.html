What this demonstrates

Grounded answers, with sources.

Real AI. Clear boundaries.

Everyday Shop, its policies and its orders are fictional examples. The agent and plugin can also read the actual historical results from the three retail projects. These are separate datasets, not records from the same shop. Skills generate drafts from what you provide. No real accounts are changed, and nothing is installed.

Messages are sent to OpenAI to generate responses. Conversation context stays in this page and clears when you start fresh or leave. The app does not save prompt text; OpenAI’s API data policies still apply. Shared daily limits keep this public demo bounded.
