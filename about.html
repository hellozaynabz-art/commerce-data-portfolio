I’m Zaynab Zennour, an analytics and data professional with more than nine years of experience across fintech, customer growth, and global supply chains.

My work connects the technical foundation with the decisions people need to make. That can mean a shared metric definition, a reliable reporting pipeline, a predictive model, or an AI workflow with clear evaluation and human review.

This portfolio brings that approach into retail and ecommerce. The projects use public historical data, reproducible code, and honest evaluation. They show how I frame a problem, make trade-offs, and turn findings into something people can explore.
